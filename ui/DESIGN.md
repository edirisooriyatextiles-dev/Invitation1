## Brand & Style

This design system draws direct inspiration from haute couture weaving, bespoke tailoring, and heritage textile ateliers. Rooted in the visual authority of rich royal navy ink, golden filament threads, and natural ivory parchment, the aesthetic balances storied artisan craftsmanship with editorial refinement.

The target audience comprises discerning clientele, bespoke tailoring patrons, textile collectors, and luxury fashion houses who prioritize provenance, tactile quality, and architectural restraint. 

The design language adopts an **Editorial Luxury & Tactile Minimalism** approach:
- Architectural precision guided by generous negative space and disciplined alignment lines reminiscent of weaver lofts and tailor pattern drafting.
- Restrained metallic warm ochre accents acting as gilded stitching across deep midnight grounds and pristine warm ivory canvases.
- High editorial typographic contrast pairing stately neoclassical serif headings with calm, balanced grotesque sans typography for metadata and transaction interfaces.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the deep, indelible character of vat-dyed navy indigo balanced against needlework gold and warm alabaster weaves.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0F1742`):** Midnight Loom Navy. Used for authoritative typography, primary action surfaces, foundational framing borders, and luxury headers.
- **Secondary (`#D99B26`):** Filament Ochre Gold. Derived from embroidery needlework, applied purposefully to brand emblems, focused indicators, bespoke badges, and featured micro-accents.
- **Tertiary (`#131C55`):** Royal Tailor Navy. Used for secondary interactive states, structural cards on dark surfaces, and subtle tonal separation.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F5`):** Warm Ivory / Unbleached Linen. Serves as the primary canvas background, conveying an organic, textured warmth far superior to sterile digital whites.

### Supporting Semantics & Surfaces
- **Surface Muted (`#F3EFE6`):** Secondary container fill for swatch panels, specification tables, and subtle compartmentalization.
- **Surface Dark Overlay (`#090D26`):** Full bleed experiential hero modules and footer architectures.
- **Text Primary (`#0F1742`):** Primary reading color on ivory surfaces, maintaining supreme optical density without using harsh absolute black.
- **Text Muted (`#585D75`):** Mid-tone slate navy for secondary specs, fiber composition labels, and metadata.
- **Gold Hover / Accent Tint (`#E5A93C`):** Radiant golden tint for interactive hover states and illuminated borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear editorial pacing:
- **Headlines & Display:** Set in **EB Garamond**, celebrating classical proportions, sharp serifs, and high-contrast stroke modulations reminiscent of carved atelier insignia and vintage bookplates.
- **Body & Controls:** Rendered in **Manrope**, offering balanced geometric clarity, open counters, and high legibility across weave specifications, order metrics, and dimensional charts.
- **Caps & Badges (`label-tag`, `label-md`):** Modeled directly on the woven label aesthetic found in couture garment collars. Always rendered in small-caps or uppercase with wide tracking (`0.08em` to `0.18em`) to mirror luxury fabric stamps and tailoring seals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure relies on a deliberate 12-column grid system calibrated to mimic fine editorial lookbooks and couture catalogs.

### Breakpoints & Grid Composition
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Content cascades linearly with vertical padding prioritizing comfortable touch targets and breathing space around brand marks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Introduces side-by-side swatch cards and dual-column editorial copy blocks.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, `margin: 4rem`, `gutter: 2.5rem`. Max container bound at `1360px` centered, ensuring wide margins that evoke high-fashion print layouts.

### Vertical Rhythm
White space is an active design element. Generous vertical gaps (`space-xl` and higher) surround hero statements and fabric macro photography, avoiding dense SaaS-style claustrophobia.

## Elevation & Depth

To remain true to tactile tailoring and fine papermaking, visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy synthetic drop shadows. Instead, depth is articulated through **Tonal Layering** and **Fine Filigree Outlines**:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Crisp Warm Ivory (`#FBF9F5`) base canvas.
- **Level 1 (Card / Container):** Tinted Natural Weave (`#FFFFFF` or `#F3EFE6`) bordered by an ultra-refined 1px stroke of Navy at 15% opacity (`rgba(15, 23, 66, 0.15)`) or soft ochre hairline (`rgba(217, 155, 38, 0.25)`).
- **Level 2 (Active / Hover / Popover):** Ambient Tailor Shadow—diffused navy tone with warm expansion: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 66, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Atelier Drawer):** Deep nocturnal lift: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(9, 13, 38, 0.22)`.
- **Ornamental Elevation:** Tailor stitching technique: Dual hairline frames (a solid inner 1px border enclosed by a secondary faint outer frame) are used for hero banners and product badge highlights, echoing the tag logo's enclosed geometric border.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts **Soft / Tailored Cornering (`roundedness: 1`)**. 

- Default interactive elements (buttons, inputs, cards) use `4px` (`0.25rem`) radii, creating crisp architectural silhouettes reminiscent of cut cloth and pressed fabric labels without harsh aggressive corners.
- Specialized badges and weave pill tags (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`) use disciplined oval contours strictly when referencing physical garment seals or buttonholes.
- Frames mimicking the trademark "Fibres • Fabrics • Fashion" insignia use sharp 90-degree outer corners combined with inward hairline offset rules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Midnight Indigo):** Background `#0F1742`, text `#FBF9F5`, font `Manrope` (weight 600, uppercase, tracking `0.08em`). Subtle border of `#0F1742`. On hover, surface shifts to `#131C55` with an ochre golden underline accent or gold bottom border glow.
- **Secondary Action (Filament Ochre):** Background `#D99B26`, text `#0F1742`, font weight 600. On hover, background deepens to `#C68A1E`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost (Tailor Line):** Transparent fill, 1px border in `#0F1742` or `#D99B26`, text matching border color. On hover, background floods with 5% opacity tint.

### Fabric Swatch & Product Cards
- Contained in off-white panels (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border in `rgba(15, 23, 66, 0.1)`.
- Image preview areas feature 1:1 or 4:5 vertical proportions, bordered by an inset 1px gold hairline separator.
- Product titles utilize `EB Garamond` (`headline-sm`), with textile composition chips positioned above in `label-tag`.

### Input Fields
- Underlined minimal style or enclosed 1px soft rectangular border (`#E0DDD5`).
- Active focus state: Border transitions to `#0F1742` with an inner 1px glow of Ochre Gold (`#D99B26`). Labels rest above in crisp Manrope uppercase tracking (`11px`, `0.1em`).

### Chips, Pills & Tags
- Modeled after bespoke garment tags. 
- Bordered in `rgba(217, 155, 38, 0.6)` with light cream background (`#FBF9F5`), text styled in `label-tag` (uppercase `EB Garamond`, tracked wide).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom squared (checkbox) or circular (radio) silhouettes in 1.5px `#0F1742` line work. Checked states reveal a centered solid Ochre Gold thread mark.

### Textile Specification Badges
- High-contrast banner units inspired by the lower framed box of the emblem: bordered with a midnight navy line (`2px`), carrying central centered dot dividers (`•`) between attributes such as "100% RAW SILK • COMBED COTTON • BESPOKE WEAVE".
